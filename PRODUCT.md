# Product facts

- OneChartLab Slides is a normal HTML presentation / slide-deck Agent Skill, not a video-production tool.
- Its first duty is to present the content clearly and make the most important point unmistakable, while conveying visual quality.
- Playback platform is not restricted; layouts should adapt well across common screen shapes.
- A deck must not become cluttered, unfocused, or more decorative than informative.
- Large-area gradients can band in screen recordings and should be avoided. Small, local gradients are neither required nor prohibited.
- Review and improve existing motion: keep useful, restrained detail; remove effects that distract, create recording defects, or have no practical purpose.
- Motion must never be required to understand the content; reduced-motion rendering must remain complete.
